THE MASTER
  2041 × 153 px  ·  aspect 13.34 : 1  ·  stroke : cap height = 1 : 7
  Average letter width 1.29 × cap  ·  letter gaps 0.35 × cap, optically spaced
  Rounded terminals. Only M, I and N carry near-full-height stems.
  The final D is a bowl, opened and mirrored. It is the signature.

  RESOLUTION CEILING
  2041 px is 1728 ppi at the 30 mm print minimum, and holds at 300 ppi
  up to roughly 173 mm wide. Beyond that — signage, vehicle livery,
  large-format — this raster is not enough. Wait for the vector.

APPROVED COLORWAYS AND GROUNDS
  Signal White     #F1EEE7  on Cosmic Black, Smoked Green, Black Cherry
                            THE DEFAULT across campaign and digital
  Crown Gold       #B8893B  on the same three. Ceremony tier.
  Mirror Silver    #BFC2C4  on the same three. Performance tier.
  Functional Black #111111  on Signal Ivory and light administrative fields

  Gold and silver are FLAT on screen. The metallic gradient is a physical
  foil, emboss or machining treatment — a property of the material, never
  of the file. Do not fake it with a gradient overlay.

MINIMUMS (pending proof)
  Clear space           0.5H each side. 1H preferred for luxury use.
  Print width           30 mm. 25 mm only after foil or engraving proof.
  Digital width         160 px.
  Positive stroke       0.20 mm or greater.
  Reversed stroke       0.30 mm or greater.
  Foil / relief stroke  0.35 mm or greater.

NEVER
  01  Stretch or compress
  02  Outline, shadow, glow or unauthorized gradients
  03  Retype in any typeface, however close
  04  Crowd below the clear space
  05  Tighten or open the tracking to fit
  06  "Correct" the mirrored D
  07  Use a colorway outside the four above
  08  Rotate, arc or set on a path
  09  Lock up permanently with a tagline
  10  Reproduce below the minimums
  11  Generate, trace or upscale with a model
  12  Use as a repeating pattern or watermark field
