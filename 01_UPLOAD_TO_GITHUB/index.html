Set it up

  2. Verify your identity and add payout and tax details when prompted.
  3. Open the product marketplace and search Supermind.
  4. Add to showcase the products you want to promote.
  5. Tag the product in every video. An untagged video cannot pay you.
  6. Pin the product on your best-performing video.

Questions people actually ask.

Not here? Once you join, ask in Help on Whop. Straight questions are answered within one business day.

Does it cost anything to join?

No fee, no minimum purchase, and no requirement to buy anything.

Do I need a big following?

No. Somebody with 4,000 followers who posts every week usually outsells somebody with 200,000 who posts twice a year. TikTok Shop has its own 1,000-follower minimum; the website code and link have none.

Can I pick my own code, and can I change it later?

You choose it in Start Here, Module 02. If it is taken we will ask for a second choice. Codes are permanent, because changing one would break every post you have already published.

Somebody bought without my code. Do I get credit?

Yes, if they clicked your link within the previous 30 days.

Why is a sale missing?

Almost always one of three things: the customer used a different discount code, the sale happened on the other platform, or the report covers different dates. Send the date, the platform and roughly the order value in Help.

How do I know when I have been promoted?

You get an email. We check weekly, your code stays the same, and the rate behind it rises.

Do I earn on my own orders?

Yes.

What does it taste like?

Like coffee. Smooth and roasted, with no earthy or mushroom-forward finish.

Will it make people jittery?

The arabica gives the lift and the L-Theanine supports calm focus alongside it. Most people find it smoother than regular coffee. Describe your own experience rather than promising theirs.

Can I use AI to make content?

Yes. Put #ai in the caption on any AI-generated visual. That is the whole requirement.

Can I run ads or host an event with Supermind?

Paid ads with your code need written permission first. Events and wholesale are welcome conversations. Ask in Help for both.

Can I use the brand's photos and logo?

Everything in The Vault and the Brand Kit is pre-approved for posts and web. Anything printed or made, such as merch or signage, needs a yes in Help first.
